# Design

Hermex's look: **calm chat, colorful objects, one voice.** The reading surface stays quiet and neutral so long agent output is easy on the eyes. Color lives on discrete *things*: a tool step, a goal, a Brain page, an approval, a Brief card. Each kind of thing always wears the same color, rendered the same way everywhere (a glow), and every screen shares one type voice (SF Pro Rounded for display) and one header, so the Brief, Goals, and chat read as one app.

## Principles

1. **Neutral ground, colored objects.** Canvases, bubbles, and body text use the `hx*` neutrals. Color appears only on an object you could tap, link, or act on.
2. **A color means a kind.** Color is assigned by object type, never by sequence, position, or status. If two things share a color, they are the same kind of thing.
3. **One material: glow.** Every colored thing is its hue on a wash of itself, deep in light mode and bright in dark mode, at two strengths: a faint `wash` for small objects (chips, request cards, drawer and log icons) and an opaque medium `card` fill for whole cards (Brief cards, the Goal hero). There are no fixed light papers anymore.
4. **Glow text is full strength.** Text in a hue is never faded; hierarchy comes from size and weight. Fading is for decorative marks only (corner icons, dividers). Faded glow fails contrast.
5. **Nous blue means interactive.** `Color.accentColor` (`#0053FD` / `#4A84FE`) is the tint for controls, the send button, links, and "Atlas is working". It is never an object color. On a Daily Brief card's paper the card's ink is the tint (`.tint(palette.ink)`), so its controls, the dictation mic included, wear the card's color.
6. **Status colors stay semantic.** `hxSuccess`, `hxWarning`, and `hxDanger` mark outcomes (done, needs care, failed), and on Money a gain or a loss. They are not object colors.

### Papers (`HermexPaper`, `Config/HermexColors.swift`)

Each paper is one hue rendered as a glow that adapts to the appearance:

- `glow`: text and symbols in the hue (the "Glow" column below).
- `wash`: the hue at 16%, behind small objects.
- `card`: a whole card's opaque fill, the hue at 20% over `hxCanvas`. **Warm hues** (`opener`, `reading`, `past`, `plain`) go 12% over `hxSurface` in dark mode instead, because warm mixed into navy reads as muddy brown.
- `edge`: a 0.5 pt hairline in the hue, so a wash or card keeps its edge on the light canvas.
- `onGlow`: text on a solid `glow` fill (a primary button).

`glow` clears 4.5:1 on both `wash` and `card` in both appearances (`DesignSystemTests` checks every paper). `paper` and `ink` are the original light index-card values, kept as each hue's source.

## Components

- **Card** (`HXCard`, `.hxCardSurface(_:)`, `HXCardRole` in `Config/HermexStyle.swift`): the one card. Its role decides the fill, edge, corners, and shadow: **glow** (one kind of thing: the paper's `card` fill and `edge`, `xl`, the `paper` shadow; Brief cards, review bites, timeline cards, Catch up, the Goal hero), **summary** (a neutral card that sits among glow cards: `hxSurface`, `hxSeparator` edge, `xl`, the `paper` shadow; the Brief opener, the review summary), **neutral** (`hxSurface`, `hxSeparator` edge, `lg`, flat; the same as `.hxCard()`), and **wash** (the paper's wash over `hxSurface` through `glowSurface`, `xl`, the `paper` shadow; Prep's run and home cards, whose controls take the paper's tint). `HXCard` stacks an optional kicker, a headline in `AppFont.display` semibold (in the glow on a colored card, `hxTextPrimary` on a neutral one, marked as a header), the body, and an optional footer, padded 16 pt; text keeps the colors its caller gives it. Cards whose words scroll inside them (Daily Deck cards, `BrainDeckCard`) use `.hxCardSurface(role, clipsContent: true)`, and a stack's bare papers fill `role.shape` with `role.fill`. Build new cards from these, never from a hand-rolled fill, stroke, and shadow. `scripts/check-design-drift` warns about fixed font sizes, numeric corner radii, raw materials, and raw colors outside `Config/` (a documented exception ends its line with `// design-drift: allow`).
- **`HXChip`** (`Config/HermexStyle.swift`): the one chip. A capsule with the wash, edge, and glow, an optional symbol, a label (caption medium, one line, middle-truncated), and an optional trailing mark. Tool calls, run counts, Brain citations, and `+N` all use it. Lay chips out with `FlowRow`, which wraps and offers an over-wide chip the row's width so large text truncates instead of overflowing.
- **Live run.** While Atlas works, each call is a `ToolCallChip`: its target (cut at 24 characters), an ellipsis while running, a red `xmark` after failing. The newest four show; older calls sit behind `+N`.
- **Finished run.** One chip per kind of tool with its count, in the order each kind first ran ("3 commands", "2 searches"), plus a red `xmark` on a kind with failures. Tapping the row opens the full log; "Expand Tools by Default" starts it open. The row is at least 44 pt tall.
- **Tool log rows.** Each row's icon sits on a 20 pt tile in its kind's wash and glow, so the log matches the chip that opened it.
- **Brain connections** (`BrainConnectionsView.swift`). A page opens on its title, then the line under it ("Concept · updated 3 wk ago" for a wiki page, whose date the server labels; "Article · Oct 6, 2026" otherwise), then its tags as one meta line ("#habits · #self": they don't act, so they aren't chips). Pages have no cover: the first screen is the page. A page with links shows them as a sideways row of linked-page cards (`wash` glow in each kind's paper, `md` corners) under the title. Above the title, every page has a Nous-blue trail: "Brain", which goes to the Brain home from any page (popping back to it, or replacing the Brain pages on top when the page was opened from chat or the Daily Brief), then the parent hub when there is one. Linked and unlinked mentions are rows on a neutral card: a kind dot, the source title, and the words around the mention with the mention marked in the page's wash and glow. A hub says so under its title ("Hub · 8 pages · updated 3 wk ago") and lists its pages, in pager order, as **In this hub** ("Pages under this one" when the hub sits in another hub) after its own words and mentions, three rows with their gists then "Show all 8"; its link cards leave those pages out, and each opens inside that hub. A page in a hub drops the hub from its link cards (the trail names it) and gets a pager capsule at the foot, opaque `hxSurface` under the glass so text never shows through, growing with Dynamic Type (at accessibility sizes it keeps the arrows and "4 of 14", the trail naming the hub) (previous, the hub and "4 of 14", next; tapping the middle lists the hub's pages in a sheet, the current one checked); the next page slides in only once it has loaded. There is no sideways swipe on the body: code blocks and tables scroll sideways there. After the mentions, Further reading is two sections: **Sources** (each with its gist) and **Also tagged** (pages sharing at least two of the page's tags, each naming them, "#rome · #person"), at most three each.
- **People list** (`BrainPeopleList.swift`). Above Everyone, in order and each hidden when empty: **Due for a catch-up**, **Birthdays soon** (next 14 days) and **Recently seen** (last 30 days), under `BrainSectionHeader`s, in the server's order. Rows are `BrainRow`s: monogram, name, the newest encounter's headline (else the relationship) on one line, and a trailing time in `meta` ("6d", "3 wk", "4 mo", "2 yr"; birthdays "today", "tomorrow", "in 11d"), in the `opener` glow on due rows. VoiceOver reads "Name, headline, seen 4 months ago, due for a catch-up". A search hides the sections. **Everyone** keeps today's rows, with a count and a relationship menu (Nous blue, "All" plus each `relationship_type` with its count) at the header's trailing edge.
- **Person page** (`BrainPersonTimelineView.swift`, when the server sends `person`). Under the header (`relationship · location · seen 6 days ago`): the **rhythm strip**, twelve 8 pt dots (scaled) for the last twelve months, oldest first, filled in the person glow for a month they were seen and `hxSeparator` otherwise, with the first and last month in `meta` at the ends (VoiceOver: "Seen in N of the last 12 months"; hidden unless the server sends twelve months); then at most three `HXChip`s in a `FlowRow` (birthday, company, else industry when there is no company or it runs over 32 characters, school; a value over 32 characters stays in About). Then **Catch up**, a glow card in the person paper (the Brief card treatment: `card` fill, `edge`, `xl`, `paper` shadow): "Catch up" in `AppFont.display` semibold in the glow, the nightly recap in `hxTextPrimary` body, then "· " lines in the glow, a birthday within 30 days first, then the open threads; hidden when there is neither recap nor line. Then **Timeline · N of M** (`HXSectionHeader`) over a `CardStack` of fixed-height cards (300 pt, scaled, cut to the visible height), newest first, each a `BrainDeckCard` drawn like a bite: an encounter in the person paper (kicker "Encounter · Oct 8 · 6 days ago" in the glow, the headline as the lead at `DeckCardText.leadStyle` sizes, then its bullets and any prose in `hxTextPrimary` body, scrolling inside the card with `moreBelowHint`; card text is parsed once per page, never per drag frame); a journal day on its own in the journal paper (kicker "Journal · Sep 25 · 3 weeks ago", the day's words as a serif lead with the name marked). A journal day with an encounter on the same date folds into that encounter as one line above the footer ("Journal: " and the day's words in serif caption, the name marked), and the card gets the footer "Open the journal day"; a journal card always has it. VoiceOver reads a card as "kicker. lead." with its notes as the value and the footer as its action. Then **About** (`HXSectionHeader`) over one neutral card (`.hxCard()`): sections in order Family, Work, School, Likes, every other profile section under its own label (first letter capitalized), Questions last, each under an `HXSectionHeader`-style title, hairlines between, and items as compact rows whose links open (a Family item like `Father: Bo Test` shows the role in secondary). A section shows three items and a "+ N more" row (the review summary's, in Nous blue since it expands) for the rest. Leftover Markdown and the mention sections follow, without the journal days already in the timeline. Older servers keep the facts grid and plain body. At the foot, on the hub pager's glass capsule (`BrainPersonActionBar`), the page acts on the person: **Text \<first name\>** (the one filled control, Nous blue; only with an `imessage:` number) opens Messages blank and logs `Texted: "…"` only once Messages reports it sent (a failed log offers Try again); **Log** opens a sheet ("What happened with …?", dictation, Save) that writes today's note through the vault's `crm log`; **Ask Atlas** starts a new chat naming the person and their file. Labels collapse to symbols for Log and Ask Atlas when they don't fit.
- **Rediscover** (`BrainRediscoverCard`): the Brain home's one glow card, a page brought back from a while ago, with Open (primary) and Another (secondary).
- **Brain home** (`BrainHomeView`): one search field that both finds and asks ("Ask or find anything"); while it has words, an **Ask Atlas** row (Nous blue, since it's a control) heads the results and starts a new chat with those words in the composer. With no query: **Ask Atlas**, suggested questions as one sideways-scrolling row of `HXChip`s (each in a 44 pt hit row), each in the paper of the module it draws on (`BrainAskSuggestion`), starting a chat the same way; **Today**, a neutral card with the Review row (its first bite's paper, "4 of 10" once started, "Done" on the summary) and up to two due catch-ups as People rows; **Browse**, a neutral card with a `BrainRow` per module (its paper's icon tile, the server's subtitle and count), then Interview prep in `knowledge` once Prep loads. Rows zoom into the screens they open.
- **Review screen** (`BrainReviewView`): today's review deck alone, filling the screen under the header ("Review", subtitle "3 of 10").
- **Bite card** (`BrainBiteCard`): one bite of the Brain's daily review, drawn like the Daily Deck's reading card (a glow card, `xl` corners, the `paper` shadow) through `BrainDeckCard`, the Brain's deck card frame that a person's timeline cards share. The paper follows the bite: ideas and takeaways wear `reading`, quotes `quote`, book highlights `book`. A kicker ("Key idea · 2 of 5", "Your takeaway", "Quote", "Highlight") sits over the lead (`DeckCardText.leadStyle` sizes; serif for quotes and highlights, with the quote mark), then the source in the glow and the detail in `hxTextPrimary`. Long text scrolls inside the card, with the shared `moreBelowHint`. A tap opens the card full screen (Open card) with what it leads to as the foot button ("Open the article", "Open the book", "Open the note"). A key idea's button opens its article with that idea expanded and tinted in the `reading` wash and edge (`BrainArticleDigestView.focusIdea`).
- **Review summary** (`BrainReviewSummaryCard`): the deck's last card is neutral (`hxSurface` fill, `hxSeparator` edge) with `xl` corners and the `paper` shadow, so it sits in the stack with the bite cards, since it summarizes the batch rather than being one kind of thing. - **Swipe stack** (`CardStack`): the Daily Deck and the Brain review share one swipe stack. Swipe left for the next card and right for the previous; only the top two cards render content and the rest show their bare paper (`stacked` shadow). Tap the top card to open it (Open card, below). - **Piece card** (`PieceArgument` in `DeckCardView.swift`): a Daily Brief card for a long video or article that has a piece map in the vault. It stays the source's own glow card (`video`, `reading`) and reading layout: the map's headline leads, the attribution is the title and its length ("Learn RAG From Scratch · 3 hours · 15 parts"), then a full-width "Open the map" (the diagram button, `OpenDiagramLabel(isMap:)`), the gist as the detail, then **The parts**: at most three of the map's steps, the ones that add most beyond the gist, drawn like an idea card's parts (`DeckPartsList`: a bullet, the step's name bold, its claim; no "Part 3" places, which live in the map), and **In its own words**: up to three checked quotes in the serif with a quote mark and their place (`DeckQuoteRow`). The map sheet is drawn by the vault from the map file with no model call (Back/Next walks the steps over a rail of the piece's parts; a part shows its gist, points and a quote), in Nous blue because it is controls.
- **Glow card** (`HXCardRole.glow`). A Daily Brief card or Goal hero: `card` fill, `edge` hairline, radius `xl`, the `paper` shadow; headline in `AppFont.display` semibold in the hue, body text in `hxTextPrimary`, everything else full-strength glow. Its buttons follow the button rules.
- **Brief opener** (`HXCardRole.summary`). A neutral card (`hxSurface`, `hxSeparator` edge) with amber (`opener` glow) accents, since it summarizes the day rather than being one kind of thing: the date and advisor, the day's title, a **Yesterday** line (the brief's `recap`, one sentence from yesterday's journal and goal check-ins; hidden when absent), then **Today · N cards**, a list of the cards ahead, each with its kind's icon on a wash tile, its title (or question), and its one-word kind (`DeckPalette.shortName`). The list is for orientation only; it isn't tappable.
- **Deck by job** (a morning deck whose cards carry `act`; older decks keep the opener above and the front dividers). The **opener** reads the day's tag lines, each after a small mark (`DeckTag.symbol`: check-in, race, due date, birthday, fixes), the date as the title, Yesterday's one, then **Today's one** on a wash panel (a small uppercase label in the glow, the pick in `AppFont.display` bold, yesterday's words behind it in italic serif with who said them, "You, to Brené Brown, Thu"), then each act ("Ideas · 2 cards") over `DeckAheadRow`s in the order Today, Ideas, Writing, Body, People; no recap and no front rows. **Act cards** drawn by the reading view (ideas, check-in, letter, a year ago) share one anatomy instead of the corner mark and the reason footnote: a **kind label** (the kind's symbol and name in footnote semibold at 0.75 of the glow, then what this one is: "Video · Idea 1 of 2", "Check-in · Roger Federer", "Letter to her · 80 days to go", "A year ago · Tue, Oct 28, 2025"), the **title** (`AppFont.display` bold; a year-ago entry's own words in the serif), the **body** (an idea's source, gist, quotes as `DeckQuoteRow`, and "The parts" as a labelled list; a check-in's or letter's thread as a small label, an italic serif quote and where it was said, then the message that leads into its question (under its speaker in footnote semibold when that isn't the card's voice, as the day's advisor on the letter)), then a hairline and **the one ask** (the question, unless it is the title, and the reflection button). Sleep, goals, Mandarin, the Ask, meals and follow-ups keep their own views. The **close** reads "Where you stand this week" over one grid (`DeckWeekGrid`): a flexible label column, seven fixed day columns under the weekday letters (today's heavy), and a fixed count column, so a long label wraps and never moves a dot; day rows sit under small uppercase front names, counted rows below a hairline as "<front> · counts". Dots and + check in live as on a front divider (`DeckRowCheckIn`). Then "Tap a ringed + to log yesterday or add one.", today's one thing, and File It. The small uppercase labels are in-card labels, not section headers.
- **Neutral card** (`.hxCard()`, `HXCardRole.neutral`): `hxSurface` with an `hxSeparator` hairline, `lg` corners. Grouped content on any screen (Settings sections, list groups, detail panels) sits in one; rows inside use `sm` corners or hairline dividers. No translucent or glass cards in content; glass is for floating controls only.
- **Section header** (`HXSectionHeader`): `AppFont.display(.subheadline, .semibold)` in sentence case above a card or rows, with an optional secondary trailing note. Never uppercase.
- **Header** (`HXNavigationTitle`, `.hxNavigationTitle(_:subtitle:)`). Every screen: a glass circle on the left (menu or back), a centered title in `AppFont.display(.subheadline, .semibold)` with an optional `caption2` subtitle (the chat's project, the Brief's "3 of 10 · Filed"), and one glass capsule of actions on the right. Chat, Daily Brief, and Goals use it.
- **Buttons.** Primary: a capsule filled with the object's `glow`, `onGlow` text (Allow, Answer, Write a reflection, File it). Secondary: a capsule in the object's `wash` and `edge`. On the app's own surfaces (no object), the primary is Nous blue. Icon actions are glass circles. `ChatDecisionButtonStyle` draws capsules.

## Shape

One radius scale, as `HXRadius`:

| Token | pt | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `xs` | 6 | Inline code, thumbnails, log icon tiles |
| `sm` | 10 | Rows, attachment tiles, buttons, drawer icon tiles |
| `md` | 14 | Inset blocks, choice buttons |
| `lg` | 18 | Section cards, action cards (approval overlay, Bot request card) |
| `xl` | 28 | Glow cards (Brief cards, Goal hero) |

Use `.continuous` corners everywhere. Chips are capsules. The composer keeps its own geometry (`ChatComposerMetrics`: a pill, and 26 pt when expanded), and the clarification card that docks on it uses the same 26 so the two read as one surface.

## Elevation

Shadows, as `.hxShadow(_:)`:

- **`paper`**: black at 0.08, radius 14, y 6, for glow cards. **`stacked`** (0.06, 10, y 4) for deck cards waiting behind the top one.
- **`float`**: black at 0.12 (light) or 0.28 (dark), radius 14, y 6, for the composer and floating glass controls.
- **`modal`**: black at 0.22, radius 18, y 12, for a card over a dimmed screen (the approval overlay).

Everything inline in chat is flat.

## Type

One voice, three faces, all Dynamic Type through `AppFont`:

- **Display: SF Pro Rounded** (`AppFont.display`): screen titles, card headlines, Brief questions, Goal titles, and headings in Atlas's replies (the Markdown theme's headings use `FontFamily(.system(.rounded))`). Semibold.
- **Body: SF Pro**: everything you read at length, controls, metadata.
- **Saved words: New York** (`AppFont.serif`), only for words a person saved: quotes, book highlights, past journal entries, and recap answers. Prep's story scripts (behind "Show script" on a story rep, and the sample telling after it) and a boss's sample answer count, because they are built from Andrew's own stories; a lesson question's worked answer is not, so it stays in the body font. Nothing else is serif.

No `.system(size:)` on text. The one exception: an SF Symbol inside a fixed-size control (a round button, a media control) keeps a fixed point size, since a growing glyph would break the control.

## Motion

A live chip fades in as its call arrives (off for replays and under Reduce Motion), and opening the log uses the chat disclosure motion. A card or chip that opens a screen is its zoom source (`.zoomSource(for:cornerRadius:)`): the screen grows out of it and shrinks back into it on the way back, with the system's crossfade under Reduce Motion. Goal cards, Brain home rows, link cards, module grid cards, and chat citation chips zoom; screens the drawer opens slide in as usual. Nothing loops or repaints continuously, with one exception: while Atlas grades a Prep answer, the submit button's sparkle breathes (a symbol effect, still under Reduce Motion) beside a once-a-second counter, because that wait runs long enough to look stuck. The Prep tutor's "Thinking" bubble uses the same sparkle until its first words stream in.

## Decisions and exceptions

- **Prep run cards are glow cards.** Every card in a Prep run (lesson, recall, result, warm-up) and the home readiness card are `HXCard(.wash(.knowledge))`, and the current-unit banner uses the same `.glowSurface(.knowledge, cornerRadius: HXRadius.xl, opaque: true)` without the shadow: the course is the object on those screens. Prep home leads with one readiness card (stories ready, graded mocks, gaps to close, the speaking streak) holding the one main action: Start call when a voice mock is due (none graded in 7 days), with today's run as an outlined Start under it; otherwise a full-width Start today's run. Then Mock interviews, Stories (three, then "See all"), the unit path, and Gaps (three, then "See all"). The path opens only the unit being taught; other open units fold to one row ("4 in reviews · boss back in a few days") until tapped, and units past the next one are one quiet "N more units" line. Path nodes never pretend to be controls: the next lesson is a heavy glow ring marked "Up next in today's run" and the boss row says when it comes up, both opening nothing; a lesson back in reviews is a neutral ring with "In reviews" (it rejoined reviews, it didn't fail). Open gaps are a filled `hxWarning` dot and improving ones an `hxWarning` ring, never red. The streak is a speaking streak: only a day with a story, probe or voice mock keeps it. XP stays off home and appears only on the day-complete screen. A run stays one rep per screen rather than a `CardStack`: the server decides the next rep only after an answer, a rep can't be revisited, and a swipe must never page away from a recall mid-answer. Missed recall key points use `hxWarning` with a ◌ icon, hits `hxSuccess` with ✓; color is never the only signal. The path's dotted connector is decorative, drawn in the paper's `edge`.
- **Prep lessons are example-first cards.** A lesson swipes sideways through up to four run cards (The case, The idea, In code, In your work), each with an "n of N · Title" kicker, page dots in the knowledge glow below, and "Try a question" pinned at the foot. The case's headline is in the glow, its body flows as one paragraph, and "Show what went wrong" (a Nous blue link) opens the lesson's full worked explanation inside the card. The idea is at most three bullets with a bold lead word. "In code" is one short artifact (a prompt, Python or SQL, at most 18 lines of 46 characters) in the chat's code view with a one-line caption in secondary text. A lesson with a vault diagram shows the Brief's full-width "Open the diagram" capsule under the cards, in the knowledge wash with its edge and glow label, opening the shared diagram sheet. "Picture it" was dropped on 2026-10-20 because its word chips only repeated the bullets. No lesson shows a script to say back.
- **The Prep tutor is chat bubbles.** After a question rep the tutor's turns sit leading on the knowledge wash (`glowSurface`) and Andrew's sit trailing on `hxSurface` with an `hxSeparator` edge; the kicker reads "Follow-up 1 of 3". "Show me" is a Nous blue link; the reply bar has the answer field, the tap-to-talk mic and Send. The end screen's headline counts follow-ups ("Got it in 2 follow-ups"), each ◌ miss carries its one-line why in secondary text, the worked answer waits behind a collapsed row, and "Go deeper in Atlas" (Nous blue) starts a chat that never changes the score.
- **Story reps show cues, not the script.** The signal is the kicker, the story title the headline in the glow, then each beat as a two-column row: a one-word label in the glow (Scope, Owned, Built, Decision, Shipped, Limit) and a short cue. The script waits behind "Show script".
- **Reward gold.** The finished-goal medal, the streak flame, the boss crown, and stars in Prep use `HermexPaper.opener.glow` as a symbol color only — never a fill, wash, card, or text — because they are marks, not objects; amber still means "waiting on you" for objects.
- **Links stay Nous blue**, `brain://` ones included: a link is a control (principle 4). The reply's Brain chips carry the Brain color. Coloring individual inline links would also mean replacing the Markdown renderer's link style.
- **Brain covers** derive their ramps from the papers (`BrainStyle.coverPaletteValues`): light is the paper, its hue, its light glow; dark is the hue at 16% and 38% over the navy canvas, then its dark glow. They stay only where the art is the object: Prep track cards and the Articles and Highlights grids. The Wiki has none (removed 2026-10-31): a cover borrowed other kinds' papers, against principle 2, and its bars motif read as a chart of numbers that don't exist. The Wiki screen pins a search field above the tag chips (like People's; it narrows to pages whose title or gist match, title matches first, with a last row that searches inside pages) and opens on **Recently updated** (the five pages touched last) before the kinds; each header counts its whole group. A wiki list row is the title, the server's gist (front matter `summary:`, else the first paragraph as plain text) and a trailing age in `meta` ("3 wk"); search, Linked from and Further reading rows lead with the kind's icon tile (`BrainModuleIcon`) or a person's monogram.
- **Bot avatar colors** (`BotAvatarColor.palette`) are server data shared with Hermes Desktop, not app chrome, so they keep Desktop's hexes.
- **Project colors** come from the server (`Color(hexString:)`) and are the user's, not the system's.
- **Translucent fills** (`systemFill` family), `tertiaryLabel`, and `placeholderText` stay UIKit: there is no `hx` equivalent and they adapt correctly.
